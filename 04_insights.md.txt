# What we learned from the data

- **Total unique customers:** 96,096  
- **Total orders:** 99,441  
- **Total revenue:** 16,008,872.12

---

## Monthly Sales Trend
- Revenue started small (only 252 in Sep 2016) and kept growing over the following months.
- For example, Feb 2017: 291,908 and Mar 2017: 449,863.
- This means sales increased a lot with time and a few months were especially strong.

---

## Top Customers by Number of Orders
- The most active customer made **17 orders**.
- Top 10 customers made between **6** and **17 orders** each.
- So, most customers buy just once or twice, but a few are loyal and buy many times.

---

## Best Selling Products
- **bed_bath_table**: 488 orders
- **furniture_decor**: 527 orders
- **garden_tools**: shows up twice in top 5, with 388 and 392 orders
- Some categories are much more popular than others.

---

## Average Order Value per Customer
- The top customer has an average order value of **13,664**.
- Several customers average **4,000 – 7,000** per order.
- Only a few customers spend as much; most spend less per order.

---

## Customer Segmentation
- Customers are grouped into three segments:
    - **New Customers**: Less than 3 orders
    - **Regular Customers**: 3 to 6 orders
    - **Loyal Customers**: More than 6 orders
- The majority of customers are "New Customers" who only buy once or twice.
- A smaller group are "Regular" and an even smaller group are "Loyal", who buy again and again.

---

## Spending & Ranking
- The highest-spending customer spent **13,664.08** in total.
- The second-highest spent **7,274.88**.
- Most of the top 10 customers spent between **4,681** and **7,274**.
- This shows that a small group of customers bring most of the revenue.

---

**Summary (Simple Words)**
- Most people buy once, but we have some regulars who bring more business.
- Only a few products/categories generate most orders.
- Some customers spend a lot more than others.
- Sales have grown quickly over time.
